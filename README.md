# Kronos Computing Library

The intelligence layer of a fusion company — an open, reproducible knowledge base on the
algorithms, machine learning, quantum computing, fusion codes, digital twins, and control
systems behind Kronos Fusion Energy.

**3,000+ pages across 40+ categories**, spanning: AI & foundations · logic and digital
systems · the fusion equations · fusion simulation codes · classical & quantum algorithms ·
interactive quantum logic gates · machine learning & ML-for-fusion · quantum computing &
helium-3 for quantum computing · the 3D model that evolves into a predictive digital twin ·
plasma & component control · HPC · security · visualization · worked examples · glossary.

Design figures are computed, reproducible targets from a published freeze — not measurements
or guarantees of performance, and nothing here is an offer of securities. No machine is built
yet (construction begins Q2 2027; first-of-a-kind operation ~2030).
